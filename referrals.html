Make a Referral to Novaedge

Most referrals arrive at an awkward moment: a discharge planned for tomorrow, or a district nurse who has noticed the tablets are not being taken. We take referrals from GPs, district nurses, therapy and discharge teams, social workers and Kirklees Council commissioners, and from adults and families referring themselves. You do not need a professional to refer you.

Get in Touch Call
To make a referral, call 07934 583993 or email hello@novaedgelimited.com. A manager answers, day or night.

What We Need From You

The Person

Name, address, telephone, date of birth and household

Assessment visit

In the person's own home, at no cost, covering needs, risks, medicines and what the person wants

Urgent hospital discharge

We assess on the ward or the same day at home, and tell you within hours if we cannot safely take it

Within two working days

Our Registered Manager or a senior assessor contacts the person or their representative to arrange a free assessment visit

Care plan and written costs

Agreed before anything starts, so nobody is invoiced for an unseen figure

If we cannot help

We say so quickly rather than holding a referral open

Referrals Arranged Through Kirklees Council

If the person has not yet had a Care Act assessment, the route is Kirklees Council adult social care on 0300 304 5555, Monday to Thursday 8am to 5pm and Friday 8am to 4.45pm, or online at my.kirklees.gov.uk, which is quickest outside office hours. The Emergency Duty Team is 07934 583993. A council assessment is free and worth doing even where someone will fund their own care.

What We Cannot Take

We support adults aged 18 to 65, adults aged 65 and over, people with a physical disability and people living with dementia, within Kirklees.

↓ We do not provide

Nursing or clinical tasks such as wound care, injections, catheter or stoma clinical management or PEG feeding, which remain with the district nursing team, and we do not provide live in care. We have applied to the Care Quality Commission for registration, and no regulated care will begin before it is granted.

Professional referrals and family enquiries go to the same number: 07934 583993, or email hello@novaedgelimited.com.
