Not Sure If We Cover You?

Ring 07934 583993 and tell us the postcode. We will give you a straight answer the same day. If we cannot reach you safely and reliably, we will say so rather than take you on and then let you down in bad weather, and we will point you towards Kirklees Council adult social care on 0300 304 5555 who can tell you who does cover your area.

Growing Carefully, Not Quickly

Our plan is to establish the service properly across Kirklees first. Extending into neighbouring parts of Calderdale, Wakefield and Bradford is something we intend to do in time, and only where the workforce, governance and quality assurance can genuinely absorb it. Any change to our registered operating area will be notified to the Care Quality Commission before it takes effect. We would rather tell you we do not cover somewhere than cover it badly.
