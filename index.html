WHO WE SUPPORT

Novaedge Services Limited is a domiciliary care provider based in the centre of Huddersfield. We support adults in their own homes across the Metropolitan Borough of Kirklees, including Huddersfield, Dewsbury, Batley, Holmfirth, Mirfield, Cleckheaton and the Colne and Holme valleys.

We support adults aged 18 to 65, adults aged 65 and over, people living with a physical disability and people living with dementia. We are a small, deliberately local service, run by two named people you can actually reach. That is not a limitation we are apologising for. It is the design. When the person answering the phone at nine in the evening is the same person who wrote your care plan, problems get solved instead of logged.

About Novaedge
Novaedge Services Limited is registered in England and Wales, company number 16962542.

Based in Huddersfield

We operate from an office in the centre of Huddersfield, and every hour of care we provide is delivered in someone's own home. No care is delivered at the office. It is where the rotas are built, the training happens and the on call phone sits.

Always Here When You Need Us

Our service is led by a Registered Manager who came into care from the front line, not from a boardroom, and who holds the Level 5 Diploma in Leadership for Health and Social Care. She is supported by a Director and Nominated Individual who holds provider level accountability for governance, finance and regulatory compliance. Between them they cover the service 24 hours a day. There is no call centre and no answering service.

We are also, quite deliberately, a digital service from day one. Every visit is checked in and checked out through electronic call monitoring, so a late or missed visit raises an alert automatically rather than waiting for someone to notice. Medicines are recorded electronically. Care plans are on a secure system that families can be given access to. None of this replaces a kind pair of hands at the door, but it means the office knows within minutes if something has gone wrong.

You will not be passed between departments. One of the two managers is on 07934 583993 at all times, including nights, weekends and bank holidays. If you ring at seven on a Sunday morning because your mother has had a fall, a manager answers.

Small, consistent care teams

We rota for familiarity, not just availability. That means fewer carers, seen more often, who know how you take your tea and which cupboard the good towels live in. Continuity is not a luxury add on. It is the single thing families tell us matters most, and it is the thing that most agencies quietly give up on first.

Paid travel time, so your visit is not cut short

Our care workers are paid for the time they spend travelling between visits, and that travel time is built into every rota. It costs us more. It is also the only honest way to make sure that the half hour you are paying for is a real half hour, and not twenty minutes because the traffic on the ring road was bad.

Care that fits who you are

Kirklees is one of the most diverse boroughs in Yorkshire, and we recruit accordingly. Food, faith, language, modesty, washing preferences, prayer times, the gender of the person helping you undress: these are not extras to be accommodated. They are the care plan.

How We Work

Our Promise to You and Your Family

Letting someone into your home is an act of trust, and we do not treat it casually. We will be honest with you about what we can and cannot do. We will tell you the price before you ask twice. We will send people you have met, who have been properly checked and properly trained. And if we get something wrong, you will hear it from us first, with an apology and a plan, not a defensive letter three weeks later.

That last part is a legal duty called the duty of candour, and we are glad it exists. But we would do it anyway, because a service that cannot admit a mistake cannot learn from one.

How quickly can care start?
−
Once our registration with the Care Quality Commission is granted, we can usually begin within a few days of your assessment, and faster where a hospital discharge is involved. The first step costs nothing and commits you to nothing, so it is worth having the conversation early rather than at the point of crisis.
Will I have the same carers every time?
+
That is the whole point of how we build our rotas. You will have a small named team rather than whoever is free. When someone is on leave or off sick we cover from within that team wherever we possibly can, and if a new face is unavoidable we introduce them rather than sending them cold.
Are you regulated?
+
We have applied to the Care Quality Commission for registration to provide personal care, and we will not deliver any regulated care before that registration is granted. Our policies, training, safeguarding arrangements and governance are already built to the standards the CQC assesses against, and we are happy to show you any of them.
